How to read this view

Ground truth is the simulator's internal temperature, not a physical sensor measurement. Agent readings change only when a read is logged. Older readings keep their timestamp and evidence ID.

The 3D model follows recorded state. Transitions interpolate visual motion; they do not run the reaction again. The agitator follows the displayed RPM continuously, including while step playback is paused. This motion does not advance the replay clock.

Each step shows the state after its recorded action or model response. No future monitor samples are used.
