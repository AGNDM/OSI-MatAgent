# Historical MCQA-only bundle receipts

`source-bundle-manifest.json` and `source-bundle-validation.json` preserve the
original version 1.0 MCQA-only distribution receipts without changing their bytes.
Their paths and sequential-placeholder statements describe that historical
release. They do not describe the current combined distribution.

The current distribution inventory and verification receipt are
`../../manifest.json` and `../../validation.json`. The MCQA questions, answer
keys and split IDs remain unchanged.
